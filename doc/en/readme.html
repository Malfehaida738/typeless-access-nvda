Typeless Access

An NVDA add-on that makes the Typeless voice dictation app on Windows easier to use for blind NVDA users. Experimental. Tested with Typeless 2.9.0. Spoken messages are in Arabic.

What it does

  • Announces when dictation starts and stops, with a short tone.
  • Reads the floating status bar shown while dictating.
  • Saves the last dictated text or answer, and can read it back or copy it.
  • Names the three unlabeled sidebar buttons: Account, Contact us and Settings, in focus mode and browse mode.
  • Reads the stats, streaks and the Check for updates text when you press Tab.
  • Offers a list of all controls with clear names.

Shortcuts that work from any program

  • NVDA+Alt+T: read the floating status bar.
  • NVDA+Alt+L: read the last saved text.
  • NVDA+Alt+C: copy the last saved text.
  • NVDA+Alt+R: turn automatic reading after dictation on or off.
  • NVDA+Alt+9: read your stats and streaks without opening the app.

Shortcuts inside the Typeless window

  • NVDA+Alt+E: list of all controls.
  • NVDA+Alt+S: turn reading of skipped text on or off.
  • Alt+1 to Alt+7: Home, History, Dictionary, Settings, Account, Contact us, Help.
  • Alt+8: Check for updates.
  • Alt+9: read your stats and streaks.

All shortcuts can be changed in NVDA menu, Preferences, Input gestures, under the Typeless category.

Changing the Typeless dictation key

The dictation key belongs to Typeless, not to this add-on. Open Typeless Settings, find Keyboard shortcuts, activate the shortcut beside Dictate, then press the key you want, for example Right Alt. Test it in a text field.
